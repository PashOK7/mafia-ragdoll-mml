### Changes since 0.1.0

- Traffic drivers slump in their seat on a fatal hit. When Tommy takes the car, his hands guide the driver's torso through the doorway. The legs respond to the seat and footwell, and the body keeps its momentum when Tommy lets go.
- Tram and train crews and passengers fall against the benches, walls and floor. Bullets move the body part they strike. A passenger pushed through a doorway can land on the street and stay there as the carriage moves on.
- All human ragdolls now use the same joint resistance. A head hanging over a bench settles after a hit.

### Installation

Close Mafia and choose one download. Both support Mafia 1.0 and 1.2.

1. **Ragdoll + MML 0.1** for a clean game. Extract the ZIP beside `Game.exe`. If your modpack already loads ASI plugins from `scripts/` and you are adding MML for the first time, extract just the `scripts/`, `mml/` and `licenses/` folders to keep your current ASI loader.
2. **Ragdoll only (if MafiaModLoader is already installed)**. Extract the ZIP beside `Game.exe`. It updates only `mml/mods/ragdoll/` and preserves your loader and other mods.

Settings are under **Options > Mods > Ragdolls**. [MafiaModLoader](https://github.com/PashOK7/MafiaModLoader)

### Tested vehicles and known limits

Driver extraction passed on the Falconer, Bolt V8 Touring and ambulance in both game builds. On the truck (`fordtru10`), the driver's pelvis can catch on the side of the cab and the torso can lag about 15 cm behind Tommy's hands. On the roadster (`forVro12`), a foot can come to rest against the wheel.
